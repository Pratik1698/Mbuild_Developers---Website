Who We Are

Our Story &
Our Mission

mBuild Developers was founded in 2016 by Er. Pramod Balasaheb Jadhav with a singular vision — to bring world-class construction quality to the towns and villages of Maharashtra. Based in Kundal, Tal. Palus, Sangli, we have grown from a small contracting firm into a trusted name across Sangli, Satara, Kolhapur, and beyond.

We believe that every family deserves a well-built home, every business deserves a strong foundation, and every community deserves infrastructure that lasts generations. This belief drives every project we undertake — from a modest residential building to landmark institutional structures like the Asha World School.

With a team of certified engineers, transparent processes, and zero compromise on quality, mBuild has earned the trust of over 200 clients and counting.

From the Founder's Desk

A Word From
Our Founder

"
When I started mBuild Developers in 2016, I had one dream — to build structures that our community would be proud of for generations. Every home we build is not just concrete and steel; it is someone's life savings, someone's dream, someone's future. That responsibility never leaves me, and it reflects in every brick we lay.
— Er. Pramod B. Jadhav, Founder & Proprietor, mBuild Developers

Er. Pramod Jadhav completed his B.E. in Civil Engineering from Shivaji University with First Class distinction. With a deep-rooted connection to the Sangli region, he channeled his technical expertise and passion for quality construction into building mBuild Developers from the ground up.

Under his leadership, the firm has successfully delivered over 250 projects — from individual residences and commercial hotels to schools, temples, water parks, and government public buildings — earning recognition from clients, consultants, and government agencies alike.

His hands-on approach, emphasis on transparency, and commitment to on-time delivery have made mBuild one of the most trusted construction names in the Sangli-Satara-Kolhapur corridor.
